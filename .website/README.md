# At Both Ends

Two unicorns. One rainbow. Every color matters.

Hold one end of a living rainbow and bring color back to a cloud-filled sky. Move your unicorn to line up each colored section with a matching creature, then hold the connection long enough to restore it. 

Dodge dark clouds, chain restores, and collect all seven colors to unleash Double Rainbow. Journey through seven colorful chapters, face The Grey, and unlock Endless mode. Give your unicorn a name, choose its colors and style, and find your rhythm in a reactive, synthesized soundtrack.

Play Solo offline with an AI companion. Online two-player co-op is available when a relay is configured.

**Controls:** WASD or arrow keys; touch and drag on mobile. P / Escape pauses Solo. M toggles sound. An interactive tutorial teaches the basics.
